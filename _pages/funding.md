We received funding from :
- CMA Maintenant is funding [the Design Enginering Center in the Institut Polytechnique de Paris](https://maintenant-dct.fr/). 
- PEPR Ensemble [funded multiple Post doc in our Team](https://www.pepr-ensemble.fr/) - The digital collaboration research program (also known as PEPR eNSEMBLE) is a national research program funded by France 2030 that brings together scientists from all disciplines to address current issues in digital collaboration. - Including the [Psephos project with Nathalie Bressa and Silvia Cazacuh]( https://www.pepr-ensemble.fr/campagne_theses_2025/00157_24-G02Q11_00-pepr-ensemble-phd-grant-2025.pdf) and Visualizing the space of debate with Toi Sin Arvidsson and Michael Baker. 
- CNRS the [MITI Mission pour les Initiatives Transverses et Interdisciplinaires](https://miti.cnrs.fr/presentation-miti/) - Funded our work on broken devices on smartphone obsolescence with Limites numerique - More here: [Sondage sur l'obsolescence des smartphones
](https://limitesnumeriques.fr/travaux-productions/sondage-obsolescence-smartphones)  with Nolwenn Maudet (ACCRA - Université de Strasbourg), Léa Mosesso (LIRIS- CNRS & Université Lyon 1), Solène Petito (LIRIS - CNRS & Université Lyon 1), Marcos Serrano (IRIT- Université Toulouse), Aurélien Tabard (LIRIS - Université Claude Bernard Lyon 1 & CNRS), Marc Teyssier (De Vinci Research Center).
- [Bpifrance](https://www.bpifrance.fr/) - Funding the Living Lab 5 G project - Banque Publique d’Investissement - SNCF has organized three Living Labs in Rennes for businesses and the general public to experiment with use cases, assess their impact on 5G, and imagine and co-construct the future of connectivity. This project funded Nathalie Bressa’s postdoctoral research.
- ANR - [Collaborative Continuity from Digital to Human](https://anr.fr/ProjetIA-21-ESRE-0030) - funding the Observation Lab - The CONTINUUM project is a collaborative research infrastructure of 30 platforms located throughout France, advancing interdisciplinary research based on interaction between computer science and the human and social sciences. Within CONTINUUM, 37 research teams develop cutting-edge research programs focusing on visualization, immersion, interaction, and collaboration, as well as on human perception, cognition, and behavior in virtual/augmented reality, with potential impact on societal issues. The infrastructure provides scientists, engineers, and industry users with a highly interconnected network of high-performance visualization and immersive platforms to observe, manipulate, understand, and share digital data, real-time multi-scale simulations, and virtual or augmented experiences. This project is funding the observation lab and the design studio.
- LVMH - with the [Chaire Entrepreneuriat Numérique Etudiant](https://www.telecom-paris-alumni.fr/fr/agenda/chaire-entrepreneuriat-numerique-etudiant-de-telecom-paristech-journee-de-l-innovation-1819) help us to create the design studio at Telecom Paris.
