I will be at [ACM CHI 2026](https://chi2026.acm.org/) in Barcelona, we are organizing a workshop on [Craft-Based Data Physicalization: Opportunities and Challenges](https://onlineacademiccommunity.uvic.ca/craftphysicalization/).
Please submit your project on the website if you are creating physicalization.
This in-person, three-hour workshop brings participants together to create, discuss, and reflect on crafting as a data practice; authoring processes and materiality; aesthetic–analytical tensions; and systems that support data craft.
We will also present the collective paper [Challenges in Synchronous & Remote Collaboration Around Visualization](https://dl.acm.org/doi/pdf/10.1145/3772318.3791117), and the workshop papers [This is not a speculation, this is the reality: Reflection on Speculative Design Workshop in HCI & Design Research](https://hal.science/hal-05554679v1/file/Huron%20CHI%202026%20WS%20This%20is%20not%20a%20speculation%20this%20is%20the%20reality.pdf) to the workshop [The Quality of Speculation Common Ground for Speculative Design in Human-Computer Interaction?](https://sites.google.com/view/qualityofspeculation/home) and [Readability as a multi-measure construct in data visualization](https://hal.science/hal-05548028/file/STAR_workshop___Measures_of_readability.pdf) to the workshop [CHI 2026 STAR Workshop-Science and Technology for Augmenting Reading](https://chi-star-workshop.github.io/).
